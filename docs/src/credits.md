# Credits

YARD is built on [ACRE2](https://github.com/IDI-Systems/acre2), created and maintained by the **ACRE2 Team** at IDI-Systems, and on the work of everyone who contributed to it: radios, signal simulation, intercoms, racks, babel, translations, Creator DLC compatibilities and documentation. Most of what's in YARD, and most of this documentation, is theirs. The [list of contributors](https://github.com/BrettMayson/yard/graphs/contributors) includes ACRE2's history.

YARD itself (the built-in voice, the Rust extension and these docs) is by the YARD Team.

YARD is not affiliated with or endorsed by the ACRE2 Team or IDI-Systems.

## Libraries

The extension uses, among others: [arma-rs](https://github.com/BrettMayson/arma-rs), [quinn](https://github.com/quinn-rs/quinn) (QUIC), [rustls](https://github.com/rustls/rustls), [Opus](https://opus-codec.org/), [nnnoiseless](https://github.com/jneem/nnnoiseless) (RNNoise), [cpal](https://github.com/RustAudio/cpal) and [tokio](https://tokio.rs/). The Longley-Rice model is a port of the public-domain NTIA Irregular Terrain Model.
